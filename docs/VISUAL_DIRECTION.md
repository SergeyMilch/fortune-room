# Fortune Room — Crystal Ball Visual Direction

## Approved direction

The Crystal Ball vertical slice uses **A — Candlelit Axis** as its primary visual direction.

Borrow the following qualities from **C — Intimate Orb**:

- make the sphere slightly larger and visually closer to the user;
- strengthen the sense of tactile proximity;
- use a simpler pedestal;
- reduce antique ornament and decorative overload.

**B — Moonlit Room** is not the ritual-screen direction. Keep it only as a reference for the future shared Fortune Room scene and its environmental depth.

## Composition

- Portrait Android composition with a calm, nearly symmetrical central axis.
- Place the sphere in the horizontal center, slightly below the screen midpoint. Target roughly 42–46% of the screen width before device testing.
- The dark wooden tabletop anchors the lower part of the screen; the background remains quiet and uncluttered.
- Place the short instruction in the clean upper third, centered above the sphere.
- Reveal the prediction inside the sphere, centered and limited to 2–3 short readable lines.
- UI remains minimal so the sphere is the unquestionable focal point.

## Palette and light

Working palette, to be refined into final tokens later:

- near-black: `#06090D`;
- deep navy: `#0B1722`;
- dark walnut: `#241610`;
- aged bronze: `#7A5A32`;
- restrained amber: `#C7924A`;
- warm parchment text: `#E7D9BE`;
- milky blue-gray smoke: `#8FA9B5`.

Use soft warm candlelight from the lower sides, with a faint cool navy fill behind and inside the sphere. Light should feel controlled, warm, calm, and premium. During the ritual, the environment may dim subtly while the internal sphere light grows.

Do not use galaxy imagery, starfields, purple neon, magenta glow, glowing runes, excessive celestial symbols, or casino/fantasy UI aesthetics.

Inside the sphere use milky blue or gray-blue smoke, a soft restrained glow, and sparse delicate particles. The effect must read as glass, haze, and light—not outer space.

## Layer strategy

Static or prepared image assets:

- quiet room background;
- dark wooden tabletop/foreground;
- simplified aged-bronze pedestal;
- crystal sphere shell;
- separate glass reflection and highlight masks;
- subtle vignette and environmental light masks;
- optional smoke/noise textures where they improve quality.

Dynamic layers and properties:

- sphere touch scale/reaction;
- internal light and glow intensity;
- smoke movement;
- sparse particles;
- reflection/highlight intensity;
- subtle environment dimming;
- restrained candlelight fluctuation;
- instruction fade and prediction reveal.

The three generated concepts are pre-production references only. They must not be used as runtime assets.
