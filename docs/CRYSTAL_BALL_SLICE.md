# Crystal Ball vertical slice — first implementation

## Composition

- calm portrait axis based on Candlelit Axis;
- sphere centered slightly below the visual midpoint and enlarged toward Intimate Orb;
- quiet instruction in the upper third;
- dark walnut surface anchors the lower 30% of the screen;
- prediction stays inside the sphere in three short lines.

## Layer boundaries

1. Static React Native scene: near-black/navy room, warm side light, walnut table.
2. Skia object layer: restrained bronze pedestal, glass shell, cool interior, smoke haze, sparse particles, reflection masks.
3. Reanimated atmosphere: environment dim, sphere scale, blue-gray aura and prediction reveal.
4. Interaction: 620 ms hold threshold, then a 2.78 second focus/gather/reveal sequence with three sparse haptic cues.

## Asset follow-up

The local concept images remain references only. After composition review, prepare separate runtime assets for a quiet room texture, walnut tabletop, glass reflection mask, subtle smoke/noise and soft environmental light masks. Keep the Skia light and interaction dynamic.
